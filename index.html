App Walkthrough Guide

Welcome to the Optimal Purchase Planner! This app helps you get the most value for your budget by calculating the best RYO (Roll-Your-Own) purchases based on current market averages or your custom prices.

1. The Calculator Tab

  • **Money Available:** Enter the exact dollar amount you plan to spend. Calculation runs automatically when you finish typing.
  • **What Do You Need?** Select which items you are missing. The app will only calculate purchases for the items you mark as **required** (green background).
  • **Calculate Best Value:** The planner determines the most efficient way to spend your budget on the required items, prioritizing the cheapest RYO unit cost (tobacco) and ensuring you have wrappers.
  • **Output:** The results show the breakdown of purchases, the **Total Spent**, **Money Remaining**, and the estimated **Total Cigarettes** you can make based on the purchase limits (whichever is lower: tobacco or wrappers).

2. Price Editing Tool (Customization)

Prices change! Use this tool to enter the **actual current prices** you pay for each item at your local shop.

  • **Saving:** Your custom prices are automatically saved to your **browser's local storage**. They will persist on this device even if you close and reopen the app.
  • **Reset:** The `Reset Prices to Local Average` button clears your custom data and restores the default prices.

3. Local Smoke Hub Tab

  • **Google Maps Search:** Use the **"Search for Tobacco Stores Near Me"** button to open a real-time Google Maps search centered on your current location.
  • **Gemini Price Search:** This feature is **disabled** as it requires a private API key for real-time web grounding.
  • **Price Ticker:** This section (visible in the Calculator tab) displays mock prices for popular pre-rolled cigarette packs nearby, based on a local average. **Clicking a pack now searches for that specific brand near your current device location.**
  • **Shops & Directions:** The list provides generic search links. **No real-world addresses are stored or displayed to protect your privacy.**
  • **Favoriting:** Click the **star icon** next to a shop to save it to your favorites in local storage.
  • **Directions:** Click the shop entry to open a Google Maps search link to nearby tobacco stores.
